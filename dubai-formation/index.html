Mainland or Freezone. Which one fits your business?

Both are UAE companies. Different rules on ownership, tax, and who you can trade with.

Freezone

Best for exports, services abroad, and IP-holding

  • 100% foreign ownership by default, no local sponsor needed.
  • 0% Corporate Tax on qualifying income if you meet the substance conditions.
  • Annual audited accounts now required by most Freezones for licence renewal.
  • Cannot invoice UAE Mainland customers directly without a local distributor.
Mainland

Best for trading with UAE customers and government

  • Trade freely across the UAE, including with mainland customers.
  • Eligible for government contracts and public tenders.
  • 9% Corporate Tax on profits above AED 375,000. Standard rate applies.
  • Auditor required every year under UAE Commercial Companies Law.
Neither route is automatically cheaper or simpler once you look past the initial licence fee. A 30-minute consultation is often enough to lock in the right one.
